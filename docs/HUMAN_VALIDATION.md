# Independent human validation protocol

No independent volunteer study has been conducted or claimed in the current benchmark. This protocol supports a small additional validation round before submission if volunteers are available.

1. Obtain a volunteer's informed agreement to record and, separately, to publish their voice. Allow participation without public release. Do not publish names or recordings without permission.
2. Use a held-out reference transcript, without showing generator labels or detector outputs.
3. Record an acceptable natural reading, then separate takes with one intentional issue: a rushed phrase, extra pause, or local energy drop. Keep microphone placement stable. A different accent or expressive choice is not automatically an error.
4. Ask two listeners to independently mark audible flaw intervals and type, blinded to the detector. Record agreement and disagreements. Resolve only with documented reasons; retain original annotations.
5. Run the unchanged app with frozen thresholds. Publish sample counts, event precision/recall, timing overlap and false positives on acceptable readings separately from synthetic results.
6. Report withheld cases, failures and out-of-scope audio. A few volunteer examples demonstrate a workflow; they do not establish population-level accuracy.

Suggested minimum practical collection: two consenting speakers, two excerpts, one acceptable and three single-flaw takes per excerpt. Keep all takes by an unseen speaker/source in the evaluation partition. The submitted public dataset can remain the archive-derived set if volunteer consent does not permit redistribution.
