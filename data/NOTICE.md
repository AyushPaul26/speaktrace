# Archive audio notices

The project software license does not replace the provenance of included archival audio. The included recordings are identified as public domain in the United States by the cited item records. No endorsement is implied.

- **John F. Kennedy, American University commencement address, June 10, 1963.** White House Audio Collection, JFKWHA-190-002, John F. Kennedy Presidential Library and Museum. [Item and explicit Public Domain status](https://www.jfklibrary.org/asset-viewer/archives/jfkwha-190-002); [official transcript](https://www.jfklibrary.org/archives/other-resources/john-f-kennedy-speeches/american-university-19630610). Short audio excerpts were made from the official downloadable MP3.
- **Ronald Reagan, Challenger address, January 28, 1986.** White House Television Office recording #242, National Archives Identifier 6014714, Ronald Reagan Library. [National Archives/DocsTeach item and public-domain statement](https://docsteach.org/document/reagan-challenger/); [official transcript](https://www.reaganlibrary.gov/archives/speech/address-nation-explosion-space-shuttle-challenger). Audio was downloaded from the [Commons OGG derivative](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ronald_Reagan_speaks_on_the_Challenger_Space_Shuttle_Disaster.ogg), attributed to the governmental Reagan Library channel and marked PD US Government.

References were cropped, converted to mono and resampled to 16 kHz. Files under `variants/` with names other than `clean.wav` contain generated gain, silence or time-stretch edits. These edits were made for engineering evaluation; they are not authentic historical deliveries or new speech by those speakers. `clean.wav` is byte-identical to its corresponding reference. Exact provenance, source checksums and download URLs are in `sources.json`; excerpt offsets are in `excerpts.json`; edit parameters and participant-time spans are in `manifest.json`.
