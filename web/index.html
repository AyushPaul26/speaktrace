BEHIND THE ANALYSIS

Evidence, with context.

SpeakTrace compares two readings of the same words. It shows how they differ, without pretending there is one right way to speak.

01

Align the words

A pretrained English speech model locates words in each recording. Poor alignment can cause scoring to be withheld.

02

Measure the difference

A fixed rubric compares local pace, pauses, and relative energy. Findings are tied to audio intervals and measurements.

03

Listen with context

Replay each interval. A flagged difference is a practice cue, not a diagnosis, emotion label, or universal quality judgment.

How recordings are processed

Connecting to check this studio’s deployment and storage policy. Analysis is enabled after the connection is verified.

How to read the evidence

Energy is relative to each recording's voiced median, not calibrated loudness. Different recording durations have separate time axes. Generated demo variations are identified as controlled examples; their labels do not enter inference.
